About Food Bridge

Our mission is to bridge the gap between food surplus and those in need.

Our Story

Food Bridge was founded with a simple vision: no food should go to waste when there are people in our communities who are hungry. We connect restaurants, grocery stores, event organizers, and individuals with surplus food to verified shelters, food banks, and community kitchens.

Our platform streamlines the donation process, making it easy for donors to share their surplus and for recipients to find and claim donations that match their needs.

Our story illustration

Our Mission

To reduce food waste and fight hunger by creating efficient connections between food donors and recipients through technology and community partnerships.
