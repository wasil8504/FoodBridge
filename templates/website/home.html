About Food Bridge

Food Bridge is a innovative platform that connects food donors with verified recipients to reduce food waste and fight hunger in our community.

Our mission is to ensure that no edible food goes to waste while ensuring that those in need have access to nutritious meals.

How It Works

  1. Donors list their surplus food with details like quantity, expiry, and pickup location.
  2. Our intelligent algorithm matches the donation with nearby verified recipients.
  3. Recipients pick up the donation, reducing waste and feeding those in need.

Our Impact

To date, we have facilitated the donation of over 10,000 meals, connected with 500+ active donors, and verified 100+ recipients across 20+ cities.
