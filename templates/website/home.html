About Food Bridge

Our Mission

Food Bridge connects food donors (restaurants, grocery stores, event organizers, individuals) with verified community recipients (shelters, NGOs, community kitchens) to reduce food waste and fight hunger.

Our Impact
  • Over 10,000 meals donated
  • 500+ active donors
  • 100+ verified recipients
  • 20+ cities served
How We Work
  1. Donors list surplus food with details
  2. Our system matches with nearby verified recipients
  3. Recipients accept and arrange pickup
  4. Food is delivered, reducing waste and feeding those in need
